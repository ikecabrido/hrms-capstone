EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

Applies To: All Employees

1. Welcome

Welcome to the organization. This Employee Handbook provides the general policies, expectations, and workplace standards that guide our employees in performing their duties and working with one another.

Every employee is expected to read, understand, and follow the policies contained in this handbook, as well as any lawful instructions, procedures, and workplace rules communicated by management.

2. Equal Opportunity and Respectful Workplace

The organization is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and inclusive workplace.

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, customers, supervisors, and business partners with professionalism, courtesy, and respect.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, threats, or other inappropriate workplace behavior will not be tolerated.

Employees who experience or witness inappropriate conduct are encouraged to report the matter through the appropriate management or Human Resources channel.

3. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:

* Perform their assigned duties responsibly and professionally.
* Follow reasonable and lawful instructions from authorized supervisors.
* Observe company policies and workplace procedures.
* Maintain honesty and integrity in all work-related activities.
* Protect company property, information, and resources.
* Maintain appropriate professional conduct.
* Cooperate with colleagues and supervisors.
* Report workplace concerns, safety issues, or policy violations promptly.

Employees are responsible for understanding the requirements of their position and asking their supervisor or Human Resources for clarification when necessary.

4. Attendance and Punctuality

Employees are expected to report to work on time and maintain reliable attendance.

Employees who are unable to report to work or expect to be late should notify their immediate supervisor or follow the organization's established attendance notification procedure as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated or unauthorized absences, tardiness, or failure to follow attendance procedures may result in appropriate corrective action.

5. Working Hours and Breaks

Employees must observe their assigned working schedule and comply with applicable break and meal-period procedures.

Any changes to an employee's schedule should be properly authorized by the appropriate supervisor.

Employees should use working time responsibly and remain available to perform assigned duties during scheduled working hours.

6. Workplace Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior while at work, while representing the organization, and when participating in organization-related activities.

The following may be subject to disciplinary action:

* Dishonesty or falsification of records.
* Theft or unauthorized use of company property.
* Threatening, abusive, or violent behavior.
* Harassment or discrimination.
* Serious insubordination.
* Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
* Deliberate damage to company property.
* Reporting to work under the influence of prohibited substances.
* Other conduct that violates company policies or applicable rules.

7. Confidentiality and Data Protection

Employees may have access to confidential company, employee, customer, or business information.

Confidential information must only be accessed, used, or disclosed for legitimate work-related purposes and in accordance with company policies.

Employees must protect passwords, accounts, documents, records, and other information entrusted to them.

Unauthorized disclosure, copying, removal, or use of confidential information may result in disciplinary action and may have other legal consequences where applicable.

8. Company Property and Resources

Company equipment, systems, documents, accounts, facilities, and other resources must be used responsibly and primarily for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must take reasonable care of company property and promptly report loss, damage, malfunction, or unauthorized access.

Company resources must not be used for unlawful, fraudulent, or unauthorized activities.

9. Information Technology and Electronic Communications

Employees must use company-provided computers, systems, networks, email accounts, applications, and other technology responsibly.

Employees must not:

* Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems or accounts.
* Share passwords or authentication credentials.
* Install unauthorized software where prohibited.
* Use company systems for unlawful activities.
* Intentionally introduce malicious software.
* Access or distribute inappropriate or prohibited content through company resources.

Employees should exercise care when opening attachments, clicking links, or responding to requests for confidential information.

10. Health, Safety, and Security

The organization is committed to maintaining a safe and secure workplace.

Employees must follow applicable safety procedures and promptly report hazards, accidents, injuries, security concerns, or unsafe conditions to the appropriate person.

Employees must cooperate with reasonable workplace safety and emergency procedures.

11. Conflict of Interest

Employees should avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the organization.

Employees should disclose potential conflicts of interest to their supervisor or the appropriate Human Resources or management representative.

Employees must not use their position, company information, or company resources for unauthorized personal gain.

12. Professional Appearance

Employees are expected to maintain an appearance appropriate to their position, responsibilities, workplace, and interactions with clients or business partners.

Where applicable, employees must follow the organization's dress code and identification requirements.

13. Social Media and Public Communications

Employees should exercise good judgment when using social media or communicating publicly about matters related to the organization.

Employees must not disclose confidential information, represent personal opinions as official company statements, or use company information or resources in an unauthorized manner.

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict lawful employee rights or communications protected by applicable law.

14. Leave and Time-Off

Employees may request leave and time off in accordance with applicable law and the organization's established procedures.

Requests should be submitted through the appropriate channel and within the required notice period whenever reasonably possible.

Employees are responsible for following applicable documentation and notification requirements.

15. Compensation and Benefits

Compensation, benefits, allowances, incentives, and other employment-related programs are governed by applicable employment agreements, company policies, and applicable law.

Employees should direct questions regarding compensation or benefits to the appropriate Human Resources representative.

16. Performance and Development

Employees are expected to perform their responsibilities according to the standards and expectations of their position.

The organization may conduct performance reviews, provide feedback, establish development goals, and implement training programs to support employee growth and organizational performance.

Employees are encouraged to communicate with their supervisors regarding work expectations, challenges, and development opportunities.

17. Discipline and Corrective Action

Violations of company policies, workplace rules, or reasonable and lawful instructions may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

Depending on the circumstances, corrective action may include coaching, written warnings, suspension, or termination of employment, subject to applicable company procedures and law.

The organization reserves the right to determine the appropriate action based on the facts and circumstances of each matter.

18. Reporting Concerns

Employees are encouraged to raise workplace concerns through their immediate supervisor, Human Resources, management, or another authorized reporting channel.

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith.

The organization will handle reports appropriately and, where applicable, maintain confidentiality to the extent reasonably possible.

19. Policy Changes

The organization may revise, update, or replace policies and procedures when necessary.

Employees are responsible for reviewing official policy updates and complying with applicable policies currently in effect.

When a revised Employee Handbook is issued, employees may be required to review and acknowledge the updated version.
