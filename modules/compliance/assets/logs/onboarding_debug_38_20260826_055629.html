This Employment Contract ("Agreement") is entered into between ________________, hereinafter referred to as the "Employee," and ________________, hereinafter referred to as the "Employer."

The Employer and Employee agree to the following terms and conditions governing the Employee's employment.

Employment Terms Notice

This Agreement sets out the principal terms and conditions of employment. It shall be read together with applicable company policies, the Employee Handbook, duly executed employment-related agreements, and applicable laws and regulations.

1.

POSITION AND DUTIES

The Employee is employed as ________________ under the ________________ department.

The Employee agrees to perform the duties and responsibilities normally associated with the position, as well as other reasonable duties that may be assigned by the Employer in accordance with the Employee's position, qualifications, and organizational requirements.

The Employee shall perform assigned duties diligently, professionally, and in accordance with the policies, procedures, rules, and standards established by the Employer.

2.

COMMENCEMENT OF EMPLOYMENT

The Employee's employment shall commence on ________________.

The Employee's date of hire is recorded as ________________.

The Employee's employment status shall be ________________, subject to the terms and conditions stated in this Agreement, applicable company policies, and applicable law.

3.

PLACE OF WORK

The Employee's primary place of work shall be at ________________, or at such other location as may reasonably be required by the Employer in connection with the Employee's duties.

Any temporary or permanent reassignment shall be made in accordance with applicable company policies and Philippine labor laws.

4.

COMPENSATION

The Employee shall receive the following compensation:

Monthly Salary: _________________
Salary in Words: _________________

In consideration of the services rendered by the Employee, the Employee shall receive a monthly salary of _________________ (_________________), subject to applicable deductions, taxes, government contributions, and other lawful deductions.

Salary shall be paid in accordance with the Employer's established payroll schedule and applicable compensation policies.

Any salary adjustment, incentive, allowance, or additional benefit shall be subject to applicable company policies, employment conditions, approval requirements, and applicable law.

5.

WORKING HOURS

The Employee shall observe the working schedule assigned by the Employer. The standard work schedule shall be determined in accordance with the requirements of the Employee's position and the Employer's operating schedule.

The Employee shall observe required attendance, timekeeping, break, overtime, and scheduling procedures established by the Employer.

Work performed beyond the applicable normal working hours shall be subject to applicable company policies and Philippine labor laws.

6.

EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

The Employee shall be entitled to benefits required by applicable Philippine laws and regulations, as well as benefits provided by the Employer under its approved policies and applicable employment arrangements.

Applicable statutory benefits may include government-mandated contributions and benefits, subject to eligibility requirements and prevailing laws and regulations.

Company-provided benefits shall be governed by the applicable policies, rules, eligibility requirements, and benefit plans of the Employer.

7.

LEAVE AND ABSENCES

The Employee shall be entitled to applicable statutory and company-provided leave benefits subject to eligibility requirements and the Employer's established leave policies.

Requests for leave shall be submitted through the prescribed procedure and within the required period, except in circumstances where advance notice is not reasonably possible.

Unauthorized or excessive absences, tardiness, or failure to comply with attendance procedures may be subject to appropriate administrative action in accordance with company policy and applicable law.

8.

EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

The Employee agrees to:

  1. Perform assigned duties competently and responsibly.
  2. Observe company policies, procedures, and workplace rules.
  3. Maintain professional conduct in the workplace.
  4. Protect company property and resources.
  5. Maintain the confidentiality of sensitive information.
  6. Comply with applicable workplace safety requirements.
  7. Maintain accurate employment and personal information.
  8. Perform duties in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

9.

CONFIDENTIALITY

The Employee acknowledges that, during employment, the Employee may have access to confidential, proprietary, personal, operational, financial, academic, administrative, or other sensitive information belonging to the Employer.

The Employee agrees not to disclose, reproduce, misuse, or provide confidential information to unauthorized persons except when disclosure is authorized by the Employer or required by law.

The Employee's confidentiality obligations shall be further governed by the applicable Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement executed in connection with employment.

10.

DATA PRIVACY AND PERSONAL INFORMATION

The Employee acknowledges that the Employer may collect, process, store, and use personal information for legitimate employment, administrative, payroll, benefits, compliance, security, and other lawful purposes.

The Employee agrees to comply with the Employer's applicable data privacy, information security, and records management policies.

11.

COMPANY PROPERTY

All equipment, documents, records, identification cards, access credentials, files, materials, and other property provided to the Employee for work purposes shall remain the property of the Employer unless otherwise stated in writing.

Upon separation from employment, the Employee shall return all company property in the Employee's possession or control, subject to applicable company procedures.

12.

WORKPLACE CONDUCT

The Employee shall maintain professional and respectful conduct and shall comply with the Employer's code of conduct, Employee Handbook, workplace policies, and applicable laws.

Conduct that violates applicable policies or laws may result in appropriate administrative action following the Employer's established procedures and applicable legal requirements.

13.

HEALTH, SAFETY, AND SECURITY

The Employee shall comply with applicable workplace health, safety, emergency, security, and access-control procedures.

The Employee shall immediately report workplace hazards, incidents, accidents, security concerns, or other matters requiring attention through the appropriate reporting channels.

14.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND WORK PRODUCT

Work products, documents, materials, records, and other outputs created by the Employee in the course of performing assigned employment duties shall be handled in accordance with applicable law, company policies, and any applicable written agreements.

The Employee shall not reproduce, distribute, commercialize, or otherwise use protected company materials outside the scope of authorized employment duties without proper authorization.

15.

COMPANY POLICIES

The Employee acknowledges that the Employer maintains policies, procedures, rules, and guidelines governing employment and workplace conduct.

The Employee agrees to familiarize themselves with and comply with applicable policies, including the Employee Handbook and other officially issued workplace policies.

16.

DISCIPLINE AND ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION

Violations of company policies, employment obligations, or applicable laws may result in appropriate administrative action in accordance with the Employer's established procedures and applicable Philippine labor laws.

Any disciplinary action shall be undertaken in accordance with applicable procedural and substantive requirements.

17.

TERMINATION AND SEPARATION

Employment may end through resignation, expiration of an applicable employment period, termination for lawful cause, or other circumstances recognized by applicable law.

The Employer and Employee shall comply with applicable notice, clearance, turnover, return-of-property, final-pay, and other separation requirements.

18.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be interpreted and implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines, including applicable provisions of the Philippine Labor Code and relevant labor and employment regulations.

Governing Legal Reference: Philippine Labor Code (PD 442)

19.

ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, together with applicable employment policies, the Employee Handbook, and other duly executed employment-related agreements, constitutes the understanding between the Employer and Employee concerning the matters covered herein, subject to applicable law.

Any amendment or modification to this Agreement shall be made in accordance with applicable company procedures and legal requirements.

20.

EMPLOYEE ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By signing below, the Employee confirms that the Employee has read and understood this Employment Contract and agrees to comply with its applicable terms and conditions, the Employee Handbook, company policies, and applicable laws.

The Employee further acknowledges receipt of the applicable onboarding documents and understands that questions concerning the terms of employment may be raised with the appropriate Human Resources representative or authorized company representative.

ELECTRONIC ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By selecting the acknowledgment option in the employee portal, the Employee confirms that they have read and understood this Employment Contract and agree to comply with its applicable terms, the Employee Handbook, company policies, and applicable laws.

EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

BESTLINK COLLEGE OF THE PHILIPPINES

Employee Policies, Workplace Standards and Guidelines

1. WELCOME

Welcome to the organization. This Employee Handbook provides the general policies, expectations, and workplace standards that guide our employees in performing their duties and working with one another.

Every employee is expected to read, understand, and follow the policies contained in this handbook, as well as any lawful instructions, procedures, and workplace rules communicated by management.

2. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY AND RESPECTFUL WORKPLACE

The organization is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and inclusive workplace.

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, customers, supervisors, and business partners with professionalism, courtesy, and respect.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, threats, or other inappropriate workplace behavior will not be tolerated.

Employees who experience or witness inappropriate conduct are encouraged to report the matter through the appropriate management or Human Resources channel.

3. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

Employees are expected to:

• Perform their assigned duties responsibly and professionally.
• Follow reasonable and lawful instructions from authorized supervisors.
• Observe company policies and workplace procedures.
• Maintain honesty and integrity in all work-related activities.
• Protect company property, information, and resources.
• Maintain appropriate professional conduct.
• Cooperate with colleagues and supervisors.
• Report workplace concerns, safety issues, or policy violations promptly.

Employees are responsible for understanding the requirements of their position and asking their supervisor or Human Resources for clarification when necessary.

4. ATTENDANCE AND PUNCTUALITY

Employees are expected to report to work on time and maintain reliable attendance.

Employees who are unable to report to work or expect to be late should notify their immediate supervisor or follow the organization's established attendance notification procedure as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated or unauthorized absences, tardiness, or failure to follow attendance procedures may result in appropriate corrective action.

5. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By selecting the acknowledgement option in the employee portal, I confirm that I have read and understood this Employee Handbook and agree to comply with the applicable policies, procedures, and workplace standards contained herein.

NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

Confidentiality, Data Protection and Information Security Agreement

CONFIDENTIALITY NOTICE

This Agreement establishes the Employee's obligations concerning confidential, proprietary, sensitive, personal, and non-public information accessed or received during the course of employment. The Employee is expected to protect such information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, removal, or distribution.

This Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between ("Company" or "Employer") and ("Employee"), effective as of .

PURPOSE

The Employee acknowledges that, in the course of employment, they may have access to confidential, proprietary, sensitive, and non-public information belonging to the Employer, its employees, students, applicants, clients, suppliers, partners, and other stakeholders.

This Agreement establishes the Employee's responsibilities in protecting such information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, or distribution.

DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:

• Employee personnel records, compensation information, employment records, and disciplinary records.
• Student records, academic records, contact information, and other personally identifiable information.
• Applicant and recruitment information.
• Business plans, strategies, financial information, budgets, and reports.
• Payroll, benefits, compensation, and government contribution information.
• Employment contracts, policies, procedures, forms, manuals, and internal documents.
• Legal documents, complaints, investigations, case records, and compliance information.
• Passwords, system credentials, access codes, database credentials, and IT security information.

EMPLOYEE OBLIGATIONS

The Employee shall:

• Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence.
• Not disclose, publish, or disseminate Confidential Information to any unauthorized person.
• Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing employment duties.
• Take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access to Confidential Information.
• Report any unauthorized disclosure or suspected breach immediately to the Human Resources Department.

RETURN OF DOCUMENTS

Upon termination of employment, the Employee shall return all documents, files, records, and materials containing Confidential Information, and shall not retain any copies in any form.

TERM

This Agreement shall remain in effect for the duration of the Employee's employment and for a period of three (3) years thereafter.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with Philippine law, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).
