Important Notice:
This Employee Handbook provides general workplace policies,
expectations, and standards applicable to employees. Employees
are responsible for reviewing and complying with applicable
company policies, procedures, lawful instructions, and workplace
rules.


1. Welcome


Welcome to the organization. This Employee Handbook provides
the general policies, expectations, and workplace standards
that guide our employees in performing their duties and
working with one another.


Every employee is expected to read, understand, and follow
the policies contained in this handbook, as well as any lawful
instructions, procedures, and workplace rules communicated
by management.


2.
Equal Opportunity and Respectful Workplace


The organization is committed to maintaining a professional,
respectful, and inclusive workplace.


Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients,
customers, supervisors, and business partners with
professionalism, courtesy, and respect.


Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, threats,
or other inappropriate workplace behavior will not be tolerated.


Employees who experience or witness inappropriate conduct
are encouraged to report the matter through the appropriate
management or Human Resources channel.


3.
Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:


  • Perform their assigned duties responsibly and professionally.

  • Follow reasonable and lawful instructions from authorized supervisors.

  • Observe company policies and workplace procedures.

  • Maintain honesty and integrity in all work-related activities.

  • Protect company property, information, and resources.

  • Maintain appropriate professional conduct.

  • Cooperate with colleagues and supervisors.

  • Report workplace concerns, safety issues, or policy violations promptly.


Employees are responsible for understanding the requirements
of their position and asking their supervisor or Human Resources
for clarification when necessary.


4.
Attendance and Punctuality


Employees are expected to report to work on time and maintain
reliable attendance.


Employees who are unable to report to work or expect to be late
should notify their immediate supervisor or follow the
organization's established attendance notification procedure
as soon as reasonably possible.


Repeated or unauthorized absences, tardiness, or failure to
follow attendance procedures may result in appropriate
corrective action.


5.
Working Hours and Breaks


Employees must observe their assigned working schedule and
comply with applicable break and meal-period procedures.


Any changes to an employee's schedule should be properly
authorized by the appropriate supervisor.


Employees should use working time responsibly and remain
available to perform assigned duties during scheduled
working hours.


6.
Workplace Conduct


Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior while
at work, while representing the organization, and when
participating in organization-related activities.

The following may be subject to disciplinary action:


  • Dishonesty or falsification of records.

  • Theft or unauthorized use of company property.

  • Threatening, abusive, or violent behavior.

  • Harassment or discrimination.

  • Serious insubordination.

  • Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.

  • Deliberate damage to company property.

  • Reporting to work under the influence of prohibited substances.

  • Other conduct that violates company policies or applicable rules.


7.
Confidentiality and Data Protection


Employees may have access to confidential company, employee,
customer, or business information.


Confidential information must only be accessed, used, or
disclosed for legitimate work-related purposes and in
accordance with company policies.


Employees must protect passwords, accounts, documents,
records, and other information entrusted to them.


Unauthorized disclosure, copying, removal, or use of
confidential information may result in disciplinary action
and may have other legal consequences where applicable.


8.
Company Property and Resources


Company equipment, systems, documents, accounts, facilities,
and other resources must be used responsibly and primarily
for legitimate business purposes.


Employees must take reasonable care of company property and
promptly report loss, damage, malfunction, or unauthorized
access.


Company resources must not be used for unlawful, fraudulent,
or unauthorized activities.


9.
Information Technology and Electronic Communications


Employees must use company-provided computers, systems,
networks, email accounts, applications, and other technology
responsibly.

Employees must not:


  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems or accounts.

  • Share passwords or authentication credentials.

  • Install unauthorized software where prohibited.

  • Use company systems for unlawful activities.

  • Intentionally introduce malicious software.

  • Access or distribute inappropriate or prohibited content through company resources.


Employees should exercise care when opening attachments,
clicking links, or responding to requests for confidential
information.


10.
Health, Safety, and Security


The organization is committed to maintaining a safe and
secure workplace.


Employees must follow applicable safety procedures and
promptly report hazards, accidents, injuries, security
concerns, or unsafe conditions to the appropriate person.


Employees must cooperate with reasonable workplace safety
and emergency procedures.


11.
Conflict of Interest


Employees should avoid situations where personal interests
conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the
organization.


Employees should disclose potential conflicts of interest to
their supervisor or the appropriate Human Resources or
management representative.


Employees must not use their position, company information,
or company resources for unauthorized personal gain.


12.
Professional Appearance


Employees are expected to maintain an appearance appropriate
to their position, responsibilities, workplace, and
interactions with clients or business partners.


Where applicable, employees must follow the organization's
dress code and identification requirements.


13.
Social Media and Public Communications


Employees should exercise good judgment when using social
media or communicating publicly about matters related to
the organization.


Employees must not disclose confidential information,
represent personal opinions as official company statements,
or use company information or resources in an unauthorized
manner.


Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict lawful
employee rights or communications protected by applicable law.


14.
Leave and Time-Off


Employees may request leave and time off in accordance with
applicable law and the organization's established procedures.


Requests should be submitted through the appropriate channel
and within the required notice period whenever reasonably
possible.


Employees are responsible for following applicable
documentation and notification requirements.


15.
Compensation and Benefits


Compensation, benefits, allowances, incentives, and other
employment-related programs are governed by applicable
employment agreements, company policies, and applicable law.


Employees should direct questions regarding compensation or
benefits to the appropriate Human Resources representative.


16.
Performance and Development


Employees are expected to perform their responsibilities
according to the standards and expectations of their position.


The organization may conduct performance reviews, provide
feedback, establish development goals, and implement
training programs to support employee growth and
organizational performance.


Employees are encouraged to communicate with their supervisors
regarding work expectations, challenges, and development
opportunities.


17.
Discipline and Corrective Action


Violations of company policies, workplace rules, or
reasonable and lawful instructions may result in corrective
or disciplinary action.


Depending on the circumstances, corrective action may include
coaching, written warnings, suspension, or termination of
employment, subject to applicable company procedures and law.


The organization reserves the right to determine the
appropriate action based on the facts and circumstances of
each matter.


18.
Reporting Concerns


Employees are encouraged to raise workplace concerns through
their immediate supervisor, Human Resources, management, or
another authorized reporting channel.


Reports should be made honestly and in good faith.


The organization will handle reports appropriately and,
where applicable, maintain confidentiality to the extent
reasonably possible.


19.
Policy Changes


The organization may revise, update, or replace policies and
procedures when necessary.


Employees are responsible for reviewing official policy
updates and complying with applicable policies currently in
effect.


When a revised Employee Handbook is issued, employees may be
required to review and acknowledge the updated version.


20. Employee Acknowledgement

I understand that I am responsible for following the
organization's policies, procedures, workplace standards,

guidance and does not replace applicable law, my employment
agreement, or specific policies and procedures issued by
the organization.


I understand that policies may be updated from time to time
and that I may be required to acknowledge updated policies
or versions of this handbook.

Purpose

use, disclosure, copying, or distribution.


2.
Definition of Confidential Information


“Confidential Information” includes, but is not limited to:


  • Employee personnel records, compensation information,
    employment records, and disciplinary records.


  • Student records, academic records, contact information,
    and other personally identifiable information.


  • Applicant and recruitment information.


  • Business plans, strategies, financial information,

  • Any other information that is reasonably understood

    as confidential.


3.
Employee Obligations


The Employee agrees to:


  1. Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential.


  2. Use Confidential Information only for legitimate
    employment-related purposes.


  3. Access confidential records only when authorized and
    when necessary to perform assigned duties.


  4. Not disclose Confidential Information to unauthorized
    persons, whether inside or outside the organization.


  5. Not copy, reproduce, photograph, download, transmit,
    publish, or distribute confidential documents without
    authorization.


  6. Protect passwords, credentials, access cards, files,
    devices, and other means of accessing Confidential
    Information.


  7. Immediately report any suspected loss, unauthorized
    access, disclosure, or security incident involving
    Confidential Information.


  8. Follow all applicable company policies and procedures
    relating to information security, privacy, records
    management, and confidentiality.


4.
Permitted Disclosure


The Employee may disclose Confidential Information only when:


  • The disclosure is specifically authorized in writing
    by the Employer.


  • The disclosure is necessary to perform legitimate
    employment duties and the recipient is authorized
    to receive the information.


  • Disclosure is required by law, regulation, court order,
    or lawful government authority.


When legally permitted, the Employee shall notify the
Employer before making a disclosure required by law so
that the Employer may take appropriate protective measures.


5.
Personal Data and Privacy


The Employee acknowledges that Confidential Information may
contain personal and sensitive personal information. The
Employee shall handle such information in accordance with
applicable data privacy laws, regulations, and the Employer's
data privacy and security policies.


The Employee shall not access, process, disclose, or retain
personal information beyond what is necessary and authorized
for the performance of their duties.


6.
Electronic and Digital Information


The Employee shall exercise reasonable care when handling
electronic Confidential Information.


This includes protecting company accounts, avoiding
unauthorized storage or transfer of files, and not using
personal devices, personal email accounts, cloud storage,
messaging applications, or external storage media for
Confidential Information unless expressly authorized.


7.
Return of Company Information


Upon termination, resignation, expiration of employment,
or whenever requested by the Employer, the Employee shall
promptly return or surrender all company property and
Confidential Information in their possession or control.


This includes physical documents, electronic files,
storage devices, access cards, company-issued devices,
credentials, copies, notes, and other materials containing
Confidential Information.


Where applicable, the Employee shall delete Confidential
Information stored on authorized personal devices or
accounts upon the Employer's instruction, subject to
applicable legal requirements.


8.
Exclusions from Confidential Information


Confidential Information does not include information that
the Employee can demonstrate:


  • Was publicly available at the time of disclosure.


  • Becomes publicly available through no unauthorized act
    or omission of the Employee.


  • Was lawfully known to the Employee before receiving it
    from the Employer.


  • Was independently developed without using Confidential
    Information.


  • Was lawfully obtained from a third party without a
    confidentiality obligation.


9.
No Unauthorized Use


The Employee shall not use Confidential Information for
personal benefit, financial gain, outside employment,
business activities, or the benefit of another person
or organization.


The Employee shall not use confidential information to
compete with, disadvantage, or otherwise cause harm to
the Employer.


10.
Intellectual Property

the Employee's employment.


Nothing in this Agreement authorizes the Employee to reproduce,
distribute, commercialize, or otherwise use such materials
outside the scope of their employment without proper
authorization.


11.
Continuing Obligation


The Employee's confidentiality obligations shall continue
after the termination, resignation, or expiration of
employment for as long as the information remains confidential
or protected by applicable law or company policy.


Trade secrets and information that by its nature requires
continuing protection shall remain subject to confidentiality
obligations for as long as such protection is legally applicable.


12.
Breach of Agreement

Compliance With Company Policies
