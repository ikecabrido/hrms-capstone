Employee Policies, Workplace Standards and Guidelines

1. WELCOME

Welcome to the organization. This Employee Handbook provides the general policies, expectations, and workplace standards that guide our employees in performing their duties and working with one another.

Every employee is expected to read, understand, and follow the policies contained in this handbook, as well as any lawful instructions, procedures, and workplace rules communicated by management.

2. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY AND RESPECTFUL WORKPLACE

The organization is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and inclusive workplace.

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, customers, supervisors, and business partners with professionalism, courtesy, and respect.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, threats, or other inappropriate workplace behavior will not be tolerated.

Employees who experience or witness inappropriate conduct are encouraged to report the matter through the appropriate management or Human Resources channel.

3. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

Employees are expected to:

• Perform their assigned duties responsibly and professionally.
• Follow reasonable and lawful instructions from authorized supervisors.
• Observe company policies and workplace procedures.
• Maintain honesty and integrity in all work-related activities.
• Protect company property, information, and resources.
• Maintain appropriate professional conduct.
• Cooperate with colleagues and supervisors.
• Report workplace concerns, safety issues, or policy violations promptly.

Employees are responsible for understanding the requirements of their position and asking their supervisor or Human Resources for clarification when necessary.

4. ATTENDANCE AND PUNCTUALITY

Employees are expected to report to work on time and maintain reliable attendance.

Employees who are unable to report to work or expect to be late should notify their immediate supervisor or follow the organization's established attendance notification procedure as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated or unauthorized absences, tardiness, or failure to follow attendance procedures may result in appropriate corrective action.

5. WORKING HOURS AND BREAKS

Employees must observe their assigned working schedule and comply with applicable break and meal-period procedures.

Any changes to an employee's schedule should be properly authorized by the appropriate supervisor.

Employees should use working time responsibly and remain available to perform assigned duties during scheduled working hours.

6. WORKPLACE CONDUCT

Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior while at work, while representing the organization, and when participating in organization-related activities.

The following may be subject to disciplinary action:

• Dishonesty or falsification of records.
• Theft or unauthorized use of company property.
• Threatening, abusive, or violent behavior.
• Harassment or discrimination.
• Serious insubordination.
• Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
• Deliberate damage to company property.
• Reporting to work under the influence of prohibited substances.
• Other conduct that violates company policies or applicable rules.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

Employees may have access to confidential company, employee, customer, or business information.

Confidential information must only be accessed, used, or disclosed for legitimate work-related purposes and in accordance with company policies.

Employees must protect passwords, accounts, documents, records, and other information entrusted to them.

Unauthorized disclosure, copying, removal, or use of confidential information may result in disciplinary action and may have other legal consequences where applicable.

8. COMPANY PROPERTY AND RESOURCES

Company equipment, systems, documents, accounts, facilities, and other resources must be used responsibly and primarily for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must take reasonable care of company property and promptly report loss, damage, malfunction, or unauthorized access.

Company resources must not be used for unlawful, fraudulent, or unauthorized activities.

9. INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY AND ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

Employees must use company-provided computers, systems, networks, email accounts, applications, and other technology responsibly.

Employees must not:

• Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems or accounts.
• Share passwords or authentication credentials.
• Install unauthorized software where prohibited.
• Use company systems for unlawful activities.
• Intentionally introduce malicious software.
• Access or distribute inappropriate or prohibited content through company resources.

Employees should exercise care when opening attachments, clicking links, or responding to requests for confidential information.

10. HEALTH, SAFETY, AND SECURITY

The organization is committed to maintaining a safe and secure workplace.

Employees must follow applicable safety procedures and promptly report hazards, accidents, injuries, security concerns, or unsafe conditions to the appropriate person.

Employees must cooperate with reasonable workplace safety and emergency procedures.

11. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Employees should avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the organization.

Employees should disclose potential conflicts of interest to their supervisor or the appropriate Human Resources or management representative.

Employees must not use their position, company information, or company resources for unauthorized personal gain.

12. PROFESSIONAL APPEARANCE

Employees are expected to maintain an appearance appropriate to their position, responsibilities, workplace, and interactions with clients or business partners.

Where applicable, employees must follow the organization's dress code and identification requirements.

13. SOCIAL MEDIA AND PUBLIC COMMUNICATIONS

Employees should exercise good judgment when using social media or communicating publicly about matters related to the organization.

Employees must not disclose confidential information, represent personal opinions as official company statements, or use company information or resources in an unauthorized manner.

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict lawful employee rights or communications protected by applicable law.

14. LEAVE AND TIME-OFF

Employees may request leave and time off in accordance with applicable law and the organization's established procedures.

Requests should be submitted through the appropriate channel and within the required notice period whenever reasonably possible.

Employees are responsible for following applicable documentation and notification requirements.

15. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

Compensation, benefits, allowances, incentives, and other employment-related programs are governed by applicable employment agreements, company policies, and applicable law.

Employees should direct questions regarding compensation or benefits to the appropriate Human Resources representative.

16. PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

Employees are expected to perform their responsibilities according to the standards and expectations of their position.

The organization may conduct performance reviews, provide feedback, establish development goals, and implement training programs to support employee growth and organizational performance.

Employees are encouraged to communicate with their supervisors regarding work expectations, challenges, and development opportunities.

17. DISCIPLINE AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

Violations of company policies, workplace rules, or reasonable and lawful instructions may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

Depending on the circumstances, corrective action may include coaching, written warnings, suspension, or termination of employment, subject to applicable company procedures and law.

The organization reserves the right to determine the appropriate action based on the facts and circumstances of each matter.

18. REPORTING CONCERNS

Employees are encouraged to raise workplace concerns through their immediate supervisor, Human Resources, management, or another authorized reporting channel.

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith.

The organization will handle reports appropriately and, where applicable, maintain confidentiality to the extent reasonably possible.

19. POLICY CHANGES

The organization may revise, update, or replace policies and procedures when necessary.

Employees are responsible for reviewing official policy updates and complying with applicable policies currently in effect.

When a revised Employee Handbook is issued, employees may be required to review and acknowledge the updated version.

NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

Confidentiality, Data Protection and Information Security Agreement

CONFIDENTIALITY NOTICE

This Agreement establishes the Employee's obligations concerning confidential, proprietary, sensitive, personal, and non-public information accessed or received during the course of employment. The Employee is expected to protect such information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, removal, or distribution.

This Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between BESTLINK College of the Philippines – Bulacan Campus ("Company" or "Employer") and Marcus Dela Peña ("Employee"), effective as of .

PURPOSE

The Employee acknowledges that, in the course of employment, they may have access to confidential, proprietary, sensitive, and non-public information belonging to the Employer, its employees, students, applicants, clients, suppliers, partners, and other stakeholders.

This Agreement establishes the Employee's responsibilities in protecting such information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, or distribution.

DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:

• Employee personnel records, compensation information, employment records, and disciplinary records.

• Student records, academic records, contact information, and other personally identifiable information.

• Applicant and recruitment information.

• Business plans, strategies, financial information, budgets, and reports.

• Payroll, benefits, compensation, and government contribution information.

• Employment contracts, policies, procedures, forms, manuals, and internal documents.

• Legal documents, complaints, investigations, case records, and compliance information.

• Passwords, system credentials, access codes, databases, and system configurations.

• Information contained in the Employer's HR, payroll, accounting, legal, compliance, and administrative systems.

• Trade secrets, proprietary processes, technical information, and internal methodologies.

• Information received from third parties under an obligation of confidentiality.

• Any other information that is reasonably understood to be confidential or that the Employer identifies as confidential.

EMPLOYEE OBLIGATIONS

The Employee agrees to:

Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential.
Use Confidential Information only for legitimate employment-related purposes.
Access confidential records only when authorized and when necessary to perform assigned duties.
Not disclose Confidential Information to unauthorized persons, whether inside or outside the organization.
Not copy, reproduce, photograph, download, transmit, publish, or distribute confidential documents without authorization.
Protect passwords, credentials, access cards, files, devices, and other means of accessing Confidential Information.
Immediately report any suspected loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, or security incident involving Confidential Information.
Follow all applicable company policies and procedures relating to information security, privacy, records management, and confidentiality.
PERMITTED DISCLOSURE

The Employee may disclose Confidential Information only when:

• The disclosure is specifically authorized in writing by the Employer.

• The disclosure is necessary to perform legitimate employment duties and the recipient is authorized to receive the information.

• Disclosure is required by law, regulation, court order, or lawful government authority.

When legally permitted, the Employee shall notify the Employer before making a disclosure required by law so that the Employer may take appropriate protective measures.

PERSONAL DATA AND PRIVACY

The Employee acknowledges that Confidential Information may contain personal and sensitive personal information. The Employee shall handle such information in accordance with applicable data privacy laws, regulations, and the Employer's data privacy and security policies.

The Employee shall not access, process, disclose, or retain personal information beyond what is necessary and authorized for the performance of their duties.

ELECTRONIC AND DIGITAL INFORMATION

The Employee shall exercise reasonable care when handling electronic Confidential Information.

This includes protecting company accounts, avoiding unauthorized storage or transfer of files, and not using personal devices, personal email accounts, cloud storage, messaging applications, or external storage media for Confidential Information unless expressly authorized.

RETURN OF COMPANY INFORMATION

Upon termination, resignation, expiration of employment, or whenever requested by the Employer, the Employee shall promptly return or surrender all company property and Confidential Information in their possession or control.

This includes physical documents, electronic files, storage devices, access cards, company-issued devices, credentials, copies, notes, and other materials containing Confidential Information.

Where applicable, the Employee shall delete Confidential Information stored on authorized personal devices or accounts upon the Employer's instruction, subject to applicable legal requirements.

EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information does not include information that the Employee can demonstrate:

• Was publicly available at the time of disclosure.

• Becomes publicly available through no unauthorized act or omission of the Employee.

• Was lawfully known to the Employee before receiving it from the Employer.

• Was independently developed without using Confidential Information.

• Was lawfully obtained from a third party without a confidentiality obligation.

NO UNAUTHORIZED USE

The Employee shall not use Confidential Information for personal benefit, financial gain, outside employment, business activities, or the benefit of another person or organization.

The Employee shall not use confidential information to compete with, disadvantage, or otherwise cause harm to the Employer.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Confidential Information may include documents, materials, systems, procedures, databases, reports, templates, processes, and other work products developed or maintained as part of the Employee's employment.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes the Employee to reproduce, distribute, commercialize, or otherwise use such materials outside the scope of their employment without proper authorization.

CONTINUING OBLIGATION

The Employee's confidentiality obligations shall continue after the termination, resignation, or expiration of employment for as long as the information remains confidential or protected by applicable law or company policy.

Trade secrets and information that by its nature requires continuing protection shall remain subject to confidentiality obligations for as long as such protection is legally applicable.

BREACH OF AGREEMENT

Unauthorized access, use, copying, disclosure, or distribution of Confidential Information may constitute a violation of company policy and may result in appropriate administrative or disciplinary action, subject to applicable law and the Employer's established procedures.

The Employer may also pursue appropriate legal remedies where permitted by law.

NO WAIVER

Failure by the Employer to immediately enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of its right to enforce that provision or any other provision in the future.

COMPLIANCE WITH COMPANY POLICIES

The Employee agrees to comply with all applicable company policies, employee handbooks, information security procedures, data privacy policies, records management procedures, and other rules relating to the protection of confidential information.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By signing this Agreement, the Employee confirms that they:

• Have read and understood this Agreement.

• Have had the opportunity to ask questions regarding its provisions.

• Understand the confidential nature of information accessed during employment.

• Agree to comply with the confidentiality obligations stated herein.

• Understand that these obligations may continue after the end of employment.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be interpreted and enforced in accordance with the applicable laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the understanding between the Employer and Employee regarding confidentiality and supplements applicable employment agreements, company policies, and other lawful obligations concerning the protection of confidential information.

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain effective to the extent permitted by law.

IMPORTANT ACKNOWLEDGMENT

This Agreement is intended to establish confidentiality obligations applicable to the Employee's employment. It should be read together with the Employee's employment agreement, employee handbook, data privacy policies, information security policies, and other applicable organizational policies.
