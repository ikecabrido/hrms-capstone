The Employee Portal includes the following features:

Secure employee login and authentication.

Employee profile management, allowing users to view and update their personal information.

Attendance monitoring, including daily time records and attendance history.

Leave management, enabling employees to submit leave requests and monitor their approval status.

Payroll viewing, allowing employees to access salary summaries, payslips, and payroll history.

Benefits and government contributions viewing (e.g., SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and tax records, if applicable).

Company announcements and notifications.

Document management for downloading HR-related forms, certificates, and company policies.

Performance evaluation viewing, where employees can access their performance appraisal results.

Training and seminar records, allowing employees to monitor completed and upcoming training activities.

Dashboard displaying employee information, recent activities, announcements, and pending requests. 

Online meeting management, admin can create meeting link where employees are able to join or share links.
