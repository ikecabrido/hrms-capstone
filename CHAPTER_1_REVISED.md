# Chapter 1: Revised Sections

## 1.2 Context and Scope

The Human Resources Employee Engagement and Relations System is designed for the Human Resource Department of Bestlink College of the Philippines – Bulacan Campus. It supports selected employee engagement and workplace relations activities, including HR announcements, employee feedback, surveys, recognition, social interaction, and grievance management.

The system provides a web-based platform for employees and authorized Human Resource personnel and administrators. Employees can access HR announcements, respond to available surveys, submit feedback, participate in supported social features, view recognition-related information, and file grievances. Authorized Human Resource personnel and administrators can manage the available announcements, surveys, feedback, recognition records, and grievance records, as well as review related dashboard information and reports.

The system organizes records produced by these activities and provides features for reviewing and monitoring information available through the platform. Its analytics are descriptive summaries of available records; they do not represent predictive or advanced analytics. The system supports selected Human Resource processes and does not replace other institutional systems or functions outside the project’s scope.

### Scope

The Human Resources Employee Engagement and Relations System includes the following functions:

**1. Centralized HR Announcements**

The system allows authorized users to create and publish employee-related announcements. Announcements can include information such as a title, category, priority, and intended audience. Employees can access announcements available to them through the system.

**2. Employee Feedback and Surveys**

The system provides an HR feedback feature for giving feedback to selected employees, along with employee satisfaction and pulse surveys. Authorized users can review available feedback and survey responses. Although some forms provide an anonymity option, the underlying records retain an employee ID; therefore, the system should not be described as guaranteeing anonymity.

**3. Employee Participation and Social Collaboration**

The system supports employee participation through available surveys and social features, including posts, forums, groups, and projects. Supported interactions include comments and reactions. The system does not provide a separate poll feature.

**4. Recognition and Rewards**

The system provides features for recording and viewing employee recognition, achievements, nominations, awards, badges, points, and available rewards. It also includes recognition-related features such as leaderboards and Employee of the Month.

**5. Grievance Management**

Employees can file grievances through the system. Within the system, grievance statuses can be updated only through the Engagement Grievance Management feature by authorized Human Resource personnel. This feature also allows them to review grievance records and record remarks, resolutions, and escalation information. The system supports reports based on available grievance records.

**6. Dashboard Overview and Monitoring**

The Dashboard Overview provides authorized Human Resource personnel and administrators with access to summaries of available employee engagement and relations information. These include records related to announcements, surveys, feedback, recognition, social posts, and grievances.

**7. Analytics and Reporting**

The system provides descriptive summaries based on available records, such as survey response counts, feedback counts by category, and grievance counts by status. The dashboard also displays social-post analysis based on keyword estimates. Grievance-related reports can be generated from the available grievance records.

The system uses user and employee information required for authentication and for associating records with users. Access to management features is intended for authorized users, according to the functions available to their accounts.

## 1.3 Problem Statement

The Human Resource Department of Bestlink College of the Philippines – Bulacan Campus handles employee engagement and workplace relations activities such as announcements, feedback, surveys, employee participation, recognition, social interaction, and grievances. Based on the initial requirements gathering, consultations, and observations conducted by the researchers, the following challenges were identified:

**1. HR Announcements and Information**

Employee-related announcements, notices, and reminders may be shared through different communication channels. As a result, employees may find it difficult to locate previous information, while Human Resource personnel may need to maintain announcement records separately.

**2. Employee Feedback and Surveys**

Feedback and survey responses may be collected through different methods. Organizing these records and reviewing them later may require additional time and effort.

**3. Employee Engagement and Participation**

Employee participation in surveys and other engagement activities needs to be recorded and reviewed. When participation information is maintained separately, authorized users may find it more difficult to monitor employee involvement.

**4. Employee Recognition**

Employee achievements, nominations, awards, and other recognition activities require organized records. When these records are maintained separately, reviewing recognition information and monitoring related activities may require additional effort.

**5. Employee Communication and Social Interaction**

Employees need accessible ways to share ideas, comment, and participate in workplace discussions. When these interactions take place through different channels, related information and participation may be more difficult to organize.

**6. Employee Grievances**

Grievances require proper documentation and follow-up. When grievance information is handled through different methods, maintaining records and monitoring their status may become difficult.

**7. Monitoring and Reporting**

Human Resource personnel and authorized administrators need organized information to monitor employee engagement, feedback, participation, recognition, and grievances. When relevant information is stored separately, reviewing records and preparing grievance-related reports may require additional effort.

These challenges indicate the need for a system that can organize the selected employee engagement and relations activities in one platform. The proposed Human Resources Employee Engagement and Relations System addresses this need through HR announcements, feedback and survey management, supported social and engagement features, recognition, grievance management, dashboard summaries, and grievance reporting.

## 1.4 Objectives and Goals

### General Objective

The general objective of this capstone project is to design, develop, and evaluate a Human Resources Employee Engagement and Relations System that supports communication, feedback management, employee participation, recognition, and grievance management at Bestlink College of the Philippines – Bulacan Campus.

### Specific Objectives

Specifically, the project aims to:

1. Develop a centralized feature for creating and publishing HR announcements, notices, reminders, and other official employee-related information.
2. Provide HR feedback and satisfaction and pulse survey features, including the review of available responses.
3. Support employee participation and social interaction through the system’s available features, such as posts, forums, groups, projects, comments, and reactions.
4. Provide recognition-related features for recording employee achievements, nominations, awards, badges, points, and available rewards.
5. Develop a grievance management feature that allows employees to file grievances and authorized Human Resource personnel to review and update grievance records.
6. Provide an administrative Dashboard Overview with descriptive summaries of available engagement and relations information, including survey, feedback, social, and grievance records.
7. Provide organized reporting for available grievance-related records.
8. Evaluate the system against the project’s identified requirements and evaluation criteria.

### Goals

The project aims to provide the Human Resource Department with a centralized system for selected employee engagement and relations activities, making related information easier to organize, review, and monitor.

The system also aims to give employees accessible ways to receive HR information, respond to surveys, provide feedback, participate in supported social features, access recognition-related information, and file grievances.

For authorized Human Resource personnel and administrators, the system aims to organize employee engagement and relations records and provide dashboard summaries and grievance reports to support monitoring and administrative review.
